## Question language standard (applies from batch 1 onward)

Founders answer badly when they have to decode the question first. Every question is
written to these rules:

- Short. One idea per question, usually under fifteen words.
- Everyday words. No "unit economics", "contribution margin", "ICP",
  "institutional memory", "escalation matrix", "yield per chair hour".
- Ask about a thing that happened, not about a concept.
- Where a number is wanted, say so plainly ("how many", "how much").
- Sounds like a person asking, not a form.

Example of the change:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "If you had to hand your next demo to someone else tomorrow, what exists today that would let them run it?" | "If someone else had to run your next sales call, what could they learn it from?" |
| "Do you know your contribution margin per outlet?" | "Do you know how much money one shop actually keeps after all its costs?" |
| "What is your rule for accepting business off a core lane?" | "When a client asks you to deliver somewhere you don't usually go, how do you decide yes or no?" |
