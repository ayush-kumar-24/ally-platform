# Exit stage — draft questions for review

**Status: draft. Nothing here is in the database.** This is for your team to read,
cut, reword and approve. Once you are happy, I turn it into a migration.

## Why this exists

A founder who picks **"Exit — preparing to hand it on or sell"** currently gets
2,414 questions, and not one of them is about exiting. We searched the whole
bank: no question mentions succession, handing over, selling the business, a
buyer, or what happens after. They get a solid general check-up and nothing
about their actual situation.

## What is here

**140 questions — 20 in each of 7 topics.** Twenty per topic so that two
founders at Exit do not get an identical diagnosis; the engine picks a handful
from each.

| # | Pillar | Topic (dimension) | Questions |
|---|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Founder Readiness | Founder Dependency / Bus Factor | 20 |
| 2 | Revenue Maturity | Revenue Concentration | 20 |
| 3 | Strategic Clarity | Institutional Memory | 20 |
| 4 | Team & Leadership | Team Structure & Role Clarity | 20 |
| 5 | Revenue Maturity | Revenue Model Clarity | 20 |
| 6 | Team & Leadership | Decision Rights | 20 |
| 7 | Product & Execution | Reliability in the Real World | 20 |

## How to read the two right-hand columns

They decide who gets asked, using the filters already built:

- **Team** — smallest team that can answer. `any` means anyone, including
  someone working alone.
- **Trading** — `yes` means it cannot be answered before the first sale.

## Language rules used

Short sentences. Ordinary words. No consultant vocabulary — no *cadence*, no
*scorecard*, no *unit economics*. Anything a founder in Indore would not say out
loud is not in here. Most questions are under 100 characters.

## 1. Founder Dependency / Bus Factor
**Pillar: Founder Readiness** — does the business actually run without you?
This is the single biggest thing a buyer pays less for.

|---|----------|------|---------|

## Notes for whoever edits this

- **Cut freely.** 20 per topic is the target so founders get variety, not a quota
  to defend. 15 good ones beat 20 with filler.
- **Keep the Team and Trading columns right.** They are what stops these reaching
  a founder who cannot answer. If you reword a question so it no longer needs a
  team, change the column too.
- **Three topics are optional extras** we did not draft: Plan-to-Vision Alignment
  (what you will do afterwards), Demand Reality (will demand hold through a
  handover) and Customer Definition (who a buyer is really buying). Say the word
  and we add 20 each.
- **These need their own home.** Exit currently shares a question bank with
  Growth, Expansion and Maturity. Dropped in as-is, a founder scaling up would
  be asked what a buyer would discount them for. Adding a separate Exit bank is
  a small change on the engineering side, done at the same time these land.
