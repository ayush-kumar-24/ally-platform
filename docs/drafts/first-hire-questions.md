# Before your first hire — draft questions for review

**20 questions for a founder who has not hired anyone yet.**

## Why this exists

A founder working alone now gets the Team & Leadership questions they can
actually answer — but at Validation, Prototype/MVP and Early Traction that is
only **15 questions**, and every one of them is about *founder dependency*: is
anyone checking your thinking, is growth capped by what you can carry.

Nothing asks the thing a solo founder actually needs help with: **when to hire,
what to hand over first, and whether they can afford it.** These 20 fill that.

## Where they sit

| | |
|---|---|
| Pillar | Team & Leadership |
| Dimension | Team Structure & Role Clarity |
| Bank | Stage 0→1 — Validation, Prototype/MVP, Early Traction |
| Team | `solo` — anyone, including a founder working alone |
| Trading | `no` — none of them needs a sale to have happened |

**Why Team Structure & Role Clarity and not Hiring Repeatability.** Before your
first hire there is no hiring process to make repeatable. What there is, is a
role to define and work to hand over — which is what Role Clarity means. It
also matters practically: Hiring Repeatability is switched off at these three
stages, so questions filed there would never reach the founders these are for.

## Questions

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| 1 | What is the first thing you would hand to someone else if you hired tomorrow? |
| 2 | What work are you doing today that you know is not worth your time? |
| 3 | Do you know what you would pay someone, and can the business afford it? |
| 4 | What would have to be true before you felt ready to hire? |
| 5 | If you hired the wrong person, how long would it take you to notice? |
| 6 | Have you written down what the job would actually involve? |
| 7 | What stops you hiring right now — money, time, or something else? |
| 8 | Is there work you keep meaning to hand over but never do? |
| 9 | Would you know how to tell a good candidate from a confident one? |
| 10 | How many hours a week would a first hire need to save you to be worth it? |
| 11 | Do you believe nobody else could do this work to your standard? |
| 12 | Who would you ask for advice before making your first hire? |
| 13 | Would you start someone on a small piece of work first, or hire straight away? |
| 14 | What would you need to write down before someone could start? |
| 15 | Is there a part of the work you would never hand over, and why? |
| 16 | Have you ever managed anyone before, in any job? |
| 17 | What would break if you took someone on and they left after a month? |
| 18 | Would you hire someone like you, or someone who fills your gaps? |
| 19 | Is waiting to hire a money decision, or does it feel like losing control? |
| 20 | If you hired someone next month, what would you want them doing by month three? |

## One thing to decide

These are tagged `solo`, which in this system means **"anyone, including a
founder working alone"** — so a founder with two or three people will see them
too. That is deliberate and mostly reads fine: *"What would you hand over
first?"* and *"Is there work you keep meaning to hand over but never do?"* are
real questions at three people.

If you want them shown **only** to founders with nobody, that needs a new
"maximum team size" setting — a small change, but a new one. Say the word.
